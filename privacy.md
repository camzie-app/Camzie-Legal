# Camzie Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 20, 2026

Camzie ("we", "us") is an app that applies AI lighting and film effects to your photos. This policy explains what information Camzie uses, why, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- You don't need an account, name, email or phone number to use Camzie.
- We don't keep your photos. The photo you upload is deleted from our servers when your edit is done, and finished edits are saved only on your phone.
- Your photos are processed by third-party AI services to create your edits, and only after you agree.
- We don't use your photos to train AI, and we don't sell your information.

## Information we use

### Photos you choose to edit

When you choose a photo and an effect, Camzie:

1. Shrinks the photo on your phone and removes its location and other hidden information.
2. Uploads it to our servers for the edit. It is deleted as soon as the edit is finished, fails, or can't start.
3. Sends it to our AI processing provider, which runs Google's **Gemini** image model to make the edit. We set our provider not to keep a record of the request, and the finished image on its servers is deleted after one hour.
4. Downloads the finished edit to your phone. We don't keep a copy.

Camzie only accesses the photos you pick. We never look through your photo library, and we only save an image to your library when you tap Save.

### An anonymous ID and your edit history

When you first open Camzie, it creates a random, anonymous ID for your app. Linked to that ID, we keep:

- your plan (Free, Plus or Pro) and when it renews;
- how many edits you have left, and a record of each edit (which effect, when, and whether it succeeded). These records contain no images.

We use these to give you your free and weekly edits, prevent misuse of free edits, and fix problems.

### Purchases

Subscriptions are bought and paid for through Apple. We never see your payment details. We use a subscription management provider: Apple and that provider share your subscription status with us (for example, which plan you bought, when it renews or ends, and your country), linked to your anonymous ID.

## Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that run Camzie, only what each needs, and only with providers that are required to protect it:

| Service | What it does | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI processing provider | Runs the AI model (Google's Gemini) that edits your photo | The photo you chose and the effect's instructions |
| Cloud hosting provider | Hosts our servers and database | Your anonymous ID, edit records, and your photo for the duration of the edit |
| Subscription management provider | Manages subscriptions | Your anonymous ID and purchase information |
| Apple | Processes payments | Purchase information (under Apple's own privacy policy) |

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Our services are based in the United States, so your information may be processed there.

## How long we keep information

- **Photos:** deleted from our servers when the edit is done. Finished edits on our AI provider's servers are deleted after one hour.
- **Anonymous ID, plan and edit records:** kept while you use Camzie, and deleted on request.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete your information:** in Camzie, open Account and tap Contact support, or email camzie.app@gmail.com, with the subject "Delete my Camzie data". We'll delete your anonymous ID, plan and edit records within 30 days. This doesn't cancel an Apple subscription; you can cancel that in your iPhone's Settings.
- **Photo access:** you can change Camzie's access to your photos at any time in your iPhone's Settings.
- Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as to access or correct your information. Contact us and we'll help.

## Children

Camzie is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Please don't upload photos of children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
